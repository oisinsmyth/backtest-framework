THE VOLUME-CONFIRMATION GOLDEN MASTER — hand arithmetic
=============================================================================
Computed by hand from the stated rules, without running the codebase. If the
engine and this file disagree, one of them is wrong about what a volume-gated
breakout backtest IS, and that argument happens here.

The point of this scenario is ONE fact, isolated: at t=9 the PRICE rule says
enter and the VOLUME rule says no, and the book stays flat. Everything else
here exists to make that fact checkable to the penny.

SETUP
-----
instrument        BTC-TEST, Equity(quantity_precision=8)   [D108]
cost stack        trade: PercentOfNotionalSpread(bps=40)  -> 0.40% of |notional|
                  no carry, no event flows => the only money leaving the book
                  is the 0.40% fee on each fill.
strategy          BreakoutStrategy(n_entry=3, n_exit=2, FixedWeight(1.0),
                                   filters=(VolumeConfirmationFilter(
                                       multiple=1.5, window=3),))
                  entry:  close(t) >  max(high) over t-3 .. t-1
                          AND volume(t) > 1.5 x mean(volume) over t-3 .. t-1
                  exit:   close(t) <  min(low)  over t-2 .. t-1
                  warm-up = max(n_entry=3, n_exit=2, filter window=3) = 3
                          -> bars 0,1,2 stand aside
fill timing       next_open (D103): a decision at bar t's close fills at bar
                  t+1's OPEN.
sizing rule       desired quantity = weight x capital / close(t), 8 dp;
                  capital = NAV at that bar's close. The order is the
                  difference against the position already held.
starting cash     100,000.00
allocator         ConstantSplitAllocator, one strategy -> capital = full NAV

BARS (daily, 00:00 UTC, days 1..11 of 2021-01)
-----------------------------------------------
NOTE ON INDEXING: t is the BAR INDEX and bar t is calendar day t+1 —
bar 0 is 2021-01-01, bar 4 is 2021-01-05. Everything below is stated in
bar indices. Mixing the two is an easy off-by-one and it is worth naming.
 t   open   high   low   close   volume
 0   100    102     98     100      100
 1   100    102     98     100      100
 2   100    102     98     100      100
 3   100    120     99     125      500     <- big price AND big volume
 4   125    126    100     100      100
 5   100    112     95      94      100
 6    94     96     90      92      100
 7    92     94     90      92      100
 8    92     94     90      92      100
 9    92    130     91     120      100     <- big price, ORDINARY volume
10   120   121    100     115      100

The two trigger bars differ in exactly one respect. Bar 3 and bar 9 both
close far above their own 3-bar high. Bar 3 prints 500 units of volume
against a 100-unit baseline; bar 9 prints 100 against the same baseline.
Nothing else about them is different, so any behavioural difference between
them is attributable to the volume rule and to nothing else.

-----------

          close 125 > 102                                    -> price says ENTER

SIZING AND FILLS
----------------
t=3  NAV = 100,000.00 (all cash, nothing held)
     desired = 1.0 x 100,000.00 / 125 = 800.00000000
     held 0 -> ORDER +800.00000000, pending

t=4  FILL +800 @ open 125
       notional = 800 x 125                        = 100,000.00
       fee      = 0.0040 x 100,000.00              =     400.00
       cash     = 100,000.00 - 100,000.00 - 400.00 =    -400.00
       position = 800
     NAV(t=4) = -400.00 + 800 x 100 = 79,600.00
     still long; desired = 1.0 x 79,600.00 / 100 = 796.00000000
     held 800 -> ORDER -4.00000000, pending
     [The 400.00 fee left cash negative, so a constant 100% WEIGHT is now
      slightly over-invested in QUANTITY terms. Trimming it is what weight
      targeting means; the trim is exactly cash/price = 4 units.]

t=5  FILL -4 @ open 100
       notional = 4 x 100                          =     400.00
       fee      = 0.0040 x 400.00                  =       1.60
       cash     = -400.00 + 400.00 - 1.60          =      -1.60
       position = 796
     NAV(t=5) = -1.60 + 796 x 94 = -1.60 + 74,824.00 = 74,822.40
     EXIT fires; desired = 0 -> ORDER -796.00000000, pending

t=6  FILL -796 @ open 94
       notional = 796 x 94                         =  74,824.00
       fee      = 0.0040 x 74,824.00               =     299.296
       cash     = -1.60 + 74,824.00 - 299.296      =  74,523.104
       position = 0
     flat; no entry -> no order

t=7  flat, no entry            -> nothing
t=8  flat, no entry            -> nothing
t=9  PRICE TRIGGERS, VOLUME REJECTS -> NO ORDER.  position stays 0.

THE ONE EPISODE
---------------

bars_held         6 - 4 = 2

                  = -100,000.00 + 400.00 + 74,824.00   = -24,776.00

net_pnl           -24,776.00 - 700.896                 = -25,476.896
                  which reconciles to the NAV change above, as it must.

WHAT THIS FILE PINS
-------------------
1. The volume baseline ENDS AT t-1 and excludes the trigger bar.
2. A trigger that clears the price rule and fails the volume rule produces NO
   order and NO cost — the book is unchanged across bar 9.
3. The filter is consulted on ENTRY ONLY. It is never asked about the exit at
   t=5, so a volume drought cannot trap an open position.
4. Fees still land on every fill, including the interior rebalancing trim.
