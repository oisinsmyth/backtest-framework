# D620 — hand file: one quarter-end per fund, typed out of the filing document itself
#
# Every number below was read by eye out of the RECORDED bytes under
# data/raw/recorder/sec_fund_filings/, in the Schedule of Investments and the Statements of
# Financial Condition of the accession named on each block — not out of the parser's output, and
# not out of any summary. The parser in scripts/build_fund_holdings_quarterly.py has to reproduce
# them. Written before that parser existed.
#
# Three accessions cover the six funds, because the four ProShares funds are series of ONE
# registrant and one 10-K carries all four:
#
#   0001193125-25-039791  ProShares Trust II   10-K  filed 2025-02-28  period 2024-12-31
#                         The PRIOR-year column and the PRIOR-year Schedule of Investments are
#                         used here (2023-12-31), which is deliberate: it is one side of the
#                         "a 10-K carries two period ends" behaviour, and it is before the
#                         repository's 2024-01-01 seal.
#   0001410578-22-002075  United States Oil Fund, LP          10-Q  filed 2022-08-05  2022-06-30
#   0001410578-24-000105  United States Natural Gas Fund, LP  10-K  filed 2024-02-29  2023-12-31
#
# CONVENTIONS
#   contracts        SIGNED. "Futures Contracts Purchased" and "Open Commodity Futures
#                    Contracts - Long" are positive; "Futures Contracts Sold" is negative.
#   contract_month   the month the document names on the line, as YYYY-MM.
#   notional_usd     the "Notional Amount at Value" column (ProShares) or the "Notional Amount"
#                    column (USCF), TYPED AS THE DOCUMENT PRINTS IT — the filings print this
#                    column unsigned even in a "Futures Contracts Sold" block. It is NOT the
#                    unrealized appreciation column beside it.
#
#                    ADDENDUM, written after the parser ran and recorded here rather than by
#                    editing a number: the FIXTURE signs `notional_usd` the same way it signs
#                    `contracts`, so on KOLD and SCO its value is the NEGATIVE of what is typed
#                    below. That is a convention and not a disagreement about a quantity, so the
#                    numbers above stay as the documents print them and the golden compares
#                    magnitudes, asserting separately that the fixture's sign matches the sign of
#                    the contract count.
#   net_assets       "Shareholders' equity" (ProShares) / "Total Partners' Capital" (USCF).
#   f_fut            futures notional / (futures notional + swap notional), over the whole fund
#                    section. Stated to six decimals with its two ingredients, so the division
#                    is checkable by hand.
