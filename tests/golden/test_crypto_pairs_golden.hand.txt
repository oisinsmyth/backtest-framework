THE CRYPTO PAIRS GOLDEN MASTER — hand arithmetic
=============================================================================
Computed by hand from the stated rules, without running the codebase. If the
engine and this file disagree, one of them is wrong about what a SHORT-ONE-LEG,
~200%-GROSS pairs backtest IS, and that argument happens here.

What is new here, and why this file exists alongside the breakout golden master
(tests/golden/test_breakout_golden.hand.txt): that scenario is long-or-flat,
never levered, and pays a trade fee and nothing else. This one is the opposite
case on every axis a pairs book differs on —

  * TWO legs fill on every decision, so a round trip pays four fees, not two;
  * one leg is SHORT, so BorrowFee (D71) charges on its notional and the other
    leg pays nothing — the shorts-only shape, not just the rate;
  * gross exposure is ~200% of NAV, so MarginInterest (D5) charges on
    max(gross - NAV, 0), a PORTFOLIO-level base no per-leg brick can see;
  * the strategy emits a target WEIGHT every bar, so the book re-normalizes to
    constant gross as NAV moves — the interior fills at t=5 are that, and they
    are the mechanism behind the study's 31x annualised turnover.

SETUP
-----
instruments       AAA, BBB — both Equity(quantity_precision=8)   [D108: spot
                  crypto as an Equity; 8-dp lot size]
cost stack        trade:            PercentOfNotionalSpread(bps=40) -> 0.40% of
                                    |quantity x price|, charged per LEG per fill
                  carry:            BorrowFee(annual_rate=0.365)
                  portfolio carry:  MarginInterest(annual_rate=0.365)
                  event flows:      none (spot crypto pays no dividends, D108)

                  0.365 / 365 = 0.001 EXACTLY per calendar day (ACT/365, D51).
                  That rate is chosen for hand arithmetic, not for realism — the
                  study itself runs 10%/yr on both (D124).

strategy          ZScorePairsStrategy(A=AAA, B=BBB, lookback=3, entry_z=1.0,
                  exit_z=0.5, leg_weight=1.0)   [D69, UNMODIFIED]
                    spread(t) = ln(AAA close) - ln(BBB close)
                    window    = the 3 spreads ending at t-1 (never t itself, D44)
                    z         = (spread(t) - mean) / sample stdev (ddof=1)
                    z >  +1.0 -> side = -1  (short AAA, long BBB)
                    z <  -1.0 -> side = +1
                    |z| < 0.5 -> flat;  0.5 <= |z| <= 1.0 -> hold current side
                    warm-up: fewer than lookback+1 = 4 visible bars -> flat
fill timing       next_open (D103): a decision at bar t's close fills at bar
                  t+1's OPEN. A decision on the final bar never fills.
starting cash     100,000.00
allocator         ConstantSplitAllocator, one strategy -> capital = full NAV,
                  recomputed from NAV every bar (D61)

AAA's closes are 100 x 1.25^t, chosen so every quantity below is exact in
binary and the z-score is a whole number. BBB is held flat so the spread is
AAA's alone; the two legs are symmetric in the code, so one moving leg is
enough to pin the arithmetic. AAA's OPENS deliberately GAP away from the prior
close at t=4 (195.3125 -> 200) and t=5 (244.140625 -> 250), so "sized on the
decision bar's close, filled at the next bar's open" is visible rather than
hidden by open == previous close.

SPREAD AND SIGNAL WALK
----------------------
L := ln(1.25) = 0.2231435513...
spread(t) = ln(100 x 1.25^t) - ln(100) = t x L exactly.

 t   n=t+1   window (spreads at)      mean   stdev   z = (tL - mean)/stdev
 0     1     n < 4  -> warm-up          -      -     flat
 1     2     n < 4  -> warm-up          -      -     flat
 2     3     n < 4  -> warm-up          -      -     flat
 3     4     t=0,1,2  ->  0, L, 2L      L      L     (3L - L)/L = +2.0
 4     5     t=1,2,3  ->  L, 2L, 3L    2L      L     (4L - 2L)/L = +2.0
 5     6     t=2,3,4  -> 2L, 3L, 4L    3L      L     (5L - 3L)/L = +2.0

   stdev of three equally-spaced values {x-d, x, x+d} with ddof=1
     = sqrt( (d^2 + 0 + d^2) / 2 ) = d,  and here d = L, so stdev = L exactly.

z = +2.0 > entry_z = 1.0 on every decision bar -> side = -1 throughout:
   target weight AAA = -1.0,  BBB = +1.0.

A steadily TRENDING spread pins z at a constant +2 — the strategy enters short
and stays short while the spread runs away from it. That is not an accident of
the fixture; it is the study's headline failure mode reproduced in six bars.

MONEY WALK

next_open fills -> views -> NAV -> targets -> sizing -> pending -> equity point.
Carry therefore sees the positions held at the PREVIOUS close, and this bar's
fills land AFTER it.

WHAT WOULD BREAK THIS FILE, AND WHAT THAT WOULD MEAN
-----------------------------------------------------
* A borrow charge on BBB   -> BorrowFee stopped being shorts-only (D71), and
                              every pairs number in the study is overstated.
* Margin base = gross      -> D5's "borrowed portion" became "whole book"; the
                              200%-gross study would double-count its financing.
* Carry charged at t=4     -> carry started accruing on positions that were not
                              yet held; next_open fills would be paying rent
                              before they existed.
* A fill at 244.140625     -> next_open fill timing silently reverted to close
                              (D103), and the strategy would be trading at the
                              price its own signal saw.
* No fills at t=5          -> the constant-gross re-normalization stopped
                              happening, and the study's turnover figure — the
                              single largest driver of its fee bill — is wrong.
