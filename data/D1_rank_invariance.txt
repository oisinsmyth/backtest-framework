VERDICT
  A weakly monotone transform of the sorting variable -- log, sqrt, z-score,
  or winsorisation at a cutoff inside the extreme bucket -- is an EXACT no-op
  for a quantile sort.  Trimming at the same cutoff is not: it deletes names,
  moves every breakpoint, and turns over part of the long leg.  The two
  branches of 'winsorise versus trim at the same cutoff' are therefore NOT
  practically equivalent for a SORT, whatever they do in a regression.
