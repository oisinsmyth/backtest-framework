Trends in Their Own Windows

The bars are partitioned. A window opens where a trend starts and closes when its gradient changes, and each window's trend is fitted on its own bars alone. Real daily candles from the 1,573-name mining fixture, log price axis — so a constant-gradient line renders straight.

The rule — dynamic windows

the bars are PARTITIONED. each window carries its own trend.

  open a window at bar s, with no gradient yet

  accumulate the pivots confirmed inside it
      (a pivot at p is knowable at p + k, never before)

  once the window holds ≥ 3 pivots, fit G once
      — that is this window's gradient

  keep re-fitting g over [s, now] as the window grows
      when |g − G| > h the trend has CHANGED:
      close the window, open the next one here

  the line = tightest offset at slope G
             over the window's own bars

Every earlier version fitted the gradient over a fixed trailing 252 bars, so a single fit could straddle two regimes and describe neither. The previous fix reset the intercept's window at a re-anchor but left the gradient still reading a year back. Now the window boundary governs both: a retired trend contributes nothing to its successor.

At a 3-pivot minimum a window earns its gradient from three points rather than five — which is also S2's own MIN_PIVOTS, so it is the one number here with precedent rather than being freshly chosen. State-on runs ~7–16%. A slope existing is not the same as a slope clearing δ for five consecutive bars; the gaps are the construction declining to name a trend, not missing data.

δ 1e-3 · min |gradient|, both slopes · 28.7%/yr h 1.56e-4 · re-anchor band · 4%/yr of slope change min pivots 3 min run 5 k 3 window dynamic, capped at 252 respects the wick fits on log price

The pivot minimum is the binding constraint, and it sets the whole character of the partition. Under a fixed 252-bar lookback a ≥5 rule voided nothing — the thinnest fit rested on 14 pivots. Under dynamic windows every window starts empty, so this number alone decides when a trend may be declared. Across the three values tried:

5 pivots → 3–4 windows a side, line on 19–30% of bars. 3 pivots → 4–6 windows, the setting drawn here. 2 pivots → 7–9 windows, line on 32–51%, but the fit becomes noisy enough that the update band stops mattering at all — the GME sweep showed windows frozen at 7/7 from 1% to 5%/yr. Three keeps h meaningful.
