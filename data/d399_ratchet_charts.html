The Ratcheted Trendline

A trendline whose gradient is sticky and whose intercept moves only when price breaks it. Drawn on real daily candles from the 1,573-name mining fixture, log price axis, so a constant-gradient line is a straight line.

The rule, as specified

hold G, the gradient. each bar:

    if |g_fresh − G| > h    RE-ANCHOR   G := g_fresh, line := the fresh OLS fit
                                       the ratchet resets

    otherwise               ADVANCE     line := line + G

                            and if price violates the line,
                            push the intercept away from price
                            exactly far enough that the trend is
                            respected again

a trend also requires  ≥ 5 pivots under the fit
                       ≥ 5 consecutive bars of state
                       otherwise: no trend here
δ 1e-3 · min |gradient|, both slopes · 28.7%/yr h 1e-3 · gradient dead band min pivots 5 min run 5 k 3 window 252 respects the wick fits on log price

Both new rules are non-binding on these six names. Over 47,220 fits the thinnest rests on 14 pivots (median 23, spanning a median 238 bars), so the ≥5-pivot rule voids nothing; and no state run was shorter than 5 bars, so the ≥5-run rule drops nothing. The gradient was never based on two bars.

What is based on one bar is the intercept. The slope comes from every pivot in the 252-bar window; the line's position is reset to a single wick at every push. The pin age stat below is how long the line has been hanging off one bar — that is the quantity your eye was catching.
