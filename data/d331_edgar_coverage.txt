NOTES FOR DOWNSTREAM
  - Dates are FILING dates. 425 / SC TO-C often precede the 8-K by a day or trail it; take the earliest per episode.
  - 8-K Item 1.01 covers every material definitive agreement (credit facilities, licences, supply deals). The
    full-text filter keeps only 8-Ks whose text matches the merger/tender/acquisition phrases; an 8-K about a
    credit agreement that mentions an earlier merger agreement will still pass. matched_queries says which.
  - Direct forms are taken by exact form type; amendments (/A) are excluded. SC TO-I (issuer self-tender) excluded.
  - Submissions list filings where the entity is the SUBJECT too (bidder-filed SC TO-T appears under the target).
  - 425 is filed by acquirers as well; a fixture name that is the BUYER also gets hits. Role is not recorded.
  - 'low' resolutions came from the ticker string appearing in filing text; verify before relying on one name.
  - EDGAR full-text search covers 2001 onward; the fixture starts 2010, so no window is truncated by it.
