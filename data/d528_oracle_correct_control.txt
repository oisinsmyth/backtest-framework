2  WHAT EACH ORACLE IS ALLOWED TO SEE

    oracle_h      traverse over [t, t+10)        OVERLAPS the trade -- knows part of the outcome
    oracle_tau    traverse over [t, t+40)       OVERLAPS the trade -- knows part of the outcome
    oracle_after  traverse over [t+40, t+50)  DISJOINT -- regime knowledge only

  Only `oracle_after` bounds what REGIME knowledge alone is worth. If it clears its
  random-matched control, a causal detector has something to aim at. If it does not, the
  regime label carries no tradeable information even when known perfectly -- and THAT is
  the honest version of ADDENDUM 8's claim.
