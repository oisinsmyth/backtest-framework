Drawn Against Fitted

The same 260 daily bars three ways: the lines drawn by hand with the whole window in view, the lines the construction fitted causally, and the two together. Identical axes throughout, log price.

drawn by hand fitted by the construction both on

The hand-drawn lines had hindsight; the fitted ones did not. This is an oracle benchmark — it bounds what a causal rule could recover and measures how close this one gets. It cannot say a causal rule should have got there, and a shortfall here refutes nothing.

The human did not call a trend everywhere either — 62% of bars, not 100%. That is the realistic ceiling, and it reframes the construction's 40% as much closer than a raw "state on 10% of bars" would suggest.
