GME at Eight Update Bands

The same 260 daily bars redrawn eight times. Only the gradient update band moves; the gradient filter is fixed throughout. Log price axis.

δ — the filter · fixed

1e-3 per bar, 28.7%/yr. Decides whether a name is trending at all: both slopes must clear it. Unchanged in every panel below.

h — the update band · swept

1% → 8%/yr, i.e. 3.9e-5 → 3.1e-4 per bar. Decides how far the window's own fitted slope must drift before the window closes and a new one opens.

the finding — under dynamic windows, h barely moves the partition

Windows: 6/5 at seven of the eight bands, 6/6 at one. Widening the update band eight-fold changes the partition almost not at all. Under a fixed 252-bar lookback the same sweep moved windows monotonically 40 → 12; once each window fits on its own bars, what ends a window is the fit becoming unstable, not the band being narrow.

State-on is the part that does respond — 8/260 at 4%/yr up to 25/260 from 5%/yr on — and it is not monotone. A wider band lets a window survive longer, which lets more of it clear the ≥5-consecutive-bar rule; but a window that survives through a genuine slope change can also stop clearing δ. h now tunes how long a trend is allowed to be wrong, not how often the line is redrawn.
