  READING IT. A mid can legitimately sit outside a thin 5-minute trade range, so the
  LEVEL of `outside` means little -- the CONTRASTS do. If the spike cells are outside far
  more often than the pool and than unchosen minutes, the cell is quote artefact and its
  apparent edge is unfillable. If they are not, suddenness is buying real prices.
