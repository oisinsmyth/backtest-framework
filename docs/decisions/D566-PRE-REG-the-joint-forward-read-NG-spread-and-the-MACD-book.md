## 5. What the read settles and what it cannot

prop book at the permitted venues.** What this read can therefore establish for the prop route is
whether the spread is worth a *vault* entry (the ledger's C-b routing) or a personal-book
component, and what its combination with the MACD arm would be worth at an account that
permitted it. The record will say which of those the numbers support and leave the routing to the
