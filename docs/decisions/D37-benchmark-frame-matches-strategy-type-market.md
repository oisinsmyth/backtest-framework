# D37 — Benchmark frame matches strategy type: market-neutral strategies benchmark against the risk-free rate, and realised beta to SPY becomes a first-class tearsheet metric with an explicit ≈0 expectation

**Status:** Committed
**Date:** 2026-07-07
**Category:** Analytics
**Source:** Session 2 — full-framework review

## Decision

Benchmark frame matches strategy type: market-neutral strategies benchmark against the risk-free rate, and realised beta to SPY becomes a first-class tearsheet metric with an explicit ≈0 expectation.

## Rationale

Beating SPY is the wrong question for a neutral book, and a nonzero realised beta falsifies the "market-neutral" claim itself. The most important diagnostic for the stated philosophy currently isn't printed.
