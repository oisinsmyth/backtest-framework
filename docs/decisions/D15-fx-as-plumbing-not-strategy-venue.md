# D15 — FX as plumbing, not strategy venue

**Status:** Committed
**Date:** 2026-07-07
**Category:** Instruments
**Source:** Session 1 — initial design review

## Decision

FX as plumbing, not strategy venue.

## Rationale

The FXPair instrument (spread + rollover carry) is needed anyway for multi-currency NAV, but retail spot-FX edges are brutal. Low priority as a trading venue.
