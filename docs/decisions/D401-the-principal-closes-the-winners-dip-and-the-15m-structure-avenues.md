session and is a different item. **Closing the avenue does not close D336, and D336's answer does not
reopen it** — a cost measurement decides whether a known small effect is deployable, not whether it
exists.

**What is NOT closed by this.** FINDINGS §52 already closes winner-selection *variants* as a family.
This closes the specific construction and the effort behind it. **The entry-timing finding itself
stands as truth** and may be cited by any successor; it is the avenue, not the fact, that is closed.
