# D38 — Sector momentum is explicitly labelled a learning/reference strategy in the repo (README + strategy docstring), not part of the portfolio thesis

**Status:** Committed
**Date:** 2026-07-07
**Category:** Analytics
**Source:** Session 2 — full-framework review

## Decision

Sector momentum is explicitly labelled a learning/reference strategy in the repo (README + strategy docstring), not part of the portfolio thesis.

## Rationale

It is directional and beta-loaded inside a framework whose stated philosophy is market-neutrality; unacknowledged, the contradiction reads as incoherence to a reviewer. One honest paragraph converts a liability into evidence of self-awareness.
