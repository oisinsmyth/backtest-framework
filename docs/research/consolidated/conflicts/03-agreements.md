# Where outside research and this repo agree

[← index](../00-INDEX.md) · siblings: [register](01-register.md) · [vs repo](02-versus-repo-measurements.md) · [inside briefs](04-inside-briefs.md)

**Why this file exists.** A record that only lists disagreements makes research look like a source of
noise. **Eleven times an outside route arrived at something this programme had already measured, or
supplied the missing half of it** — and two of those became sections of [`FINDINGS.md`](../../../FINDINGS.md)
after being **re-derived here**, which is the only way anything from this tree ever moves.

---

## A1 · `33.8 bp/side` is the cost-honest literature's own number

`[EXT]` `K6` (R6): the cost-honest literature measures the average effective spread paid by **204
academic anomaly implementations post-2005 at 68 bp**, and charges **half per trade: 34 bp/side.** It
separately measures **67 bp paid against 16 bp for the average NYSE stock** — a factor of four,
because that is what an anomaly portfolio actually trades.

`[REPO]` D285 measured **33.8 bp/side** on the names actually held.

**This reverses the premise five rounds were reasoning from.** *"Any lane rejected because this
programme's costs looked unusually high was rejected on a false premise."* — and the record says the
false premise was the commissioner's own. **Read with [R1](02-versus-repo-measurements.md#r1--the-cost-bar-every-research-round-reasons-from-is-a-superseded-statistic):
33.8 is a superseded statistic here, which makes the agreement an agreement about the level, not
about the convention.**

## A2 · Two independent routes to the same spread, agreeing to 2%

`[EXT]` `K5` (R6) inverted the verified Blume–Stambaugh closed form on `J3`'s measured equal-weight
bias: **+0.30%/yr implies 34.5 bp/side.** `[REPO]` **33.8**. **Agreement to 2%, from bid-ask bounce —
a mechanism with nothing in common with a range-based spread estimator.** Two briefs, one
cross-validation, neither commissioned to check the other.

## A7 · Breadth is the binding constraint, from both sides

`[REPO]` [FINDINGS §4](../../../FINDINGS.md): *"Breadth is the recurring binding constraint, and
headcount cannot fix it"*, with **10.06 effective independent instruments over 1,573 names**.
`[EXT]` `shorts/04` §6: at an effective breadth near **2.2**, clearing a >10%/yr net target needs an
information coefficient of **0.25–0.42** against a realistic ceiling near **0.05**; AQR's flagship
Equity Market Neutral fund has returned **7.30%/yr since Oct 2014**.

**Same constraint, two universes, arrived at independently.** And `[EXT]` `H6` closes the loop on the
repo's own figure: **~10 effective instruments across 1,573 names is exactly ρ̄ = 0.0994** under the
design-effect formula — internally consistent.

## A8 · The short leg is where the alpha is, and it is where we cannot trade

`[REPO]` [FINDINGS §19](../../../FINDINGS.md): *"The short side of this programme is a cost failure, and
neither sizing nor borrow changes the verdict"*; [§30](../../../FINDINGS.md): every short signal beats
its own names at random times **and still loses** — the names it shorts rise.

`[EXT]` Three independent results — Asquith–Pathak–Ritter, Hou–Xue–Zhang, Muravyev–Pearson–Pollet —
**each separately eliminate the entire short-leg premium** once you restrict to value-weighted,
large-cap, cheap-to-borrow names. Across **162 anomalies: +0.14%/month gross, entirely from the short
leg, −0.01%/month after borrow fees**, and *not profitable even before fees* once the high-fee 12% of
stock-dates are excluded.

**Different mechanism, identical verdict.** The repo says we cannot afford it; the literature says it
was never in the names we can reach. Detail: [signals/07](../signals/07-short-side-cross-sectional.md).

## A10 · The draft-versus-published hazard is real, and it bit us too

`[EXT]` Caught **four times** in other people's work — including a paper whose abstract **flips sign**
between draft and publication (+0.02% → −0.01%), and a working paper reporting that turnover predicts
post-2005 net returns where **the published version says no predictor is reliable and turnover enters
with the wrong sign — and the draft is the freely-indexed one.**

`[REPO-adjacent]` **The campaign then made the same error in its own record**, collapsing a 6 May 2014
working paper into the published 2015 article; caught by round 4 and corrected in place. **The class
generalises: assume it, do not discover it.**
