## The number under both of them `[EXT]` `K6`

**Post-2005, top 90% of market cap, ~200 published anomalies:**

```
MEDIAN 7 bp/month GROSS, MEDIAN t = 0.45     against 48 bp in the all-stock / pre-2006 cell
Var(t) = 1.09 vs a luck-only 1.00            ->  signal share 0.08
best survivor  66 bp -> 6 bp                 a second study reaches 6 bp BY A DIFFERENT ROUTE
~85% of published alpha required BOTH pre-2006 data AND microcaps
```

> **A survival rate is a statement about a screening rule. `Var(t)` is a statement about how much
> signal is in the whole cross-section, and it does not depend on picking a threshold.** That is why
> `K6` reports the second and refuses to arbitrate the first.

## Construction dispersion IS multiple testing, counted differently `[EXT]` `C2` §7

**The non-standard-errors literature joins the two explicitly**, and the join is the important part:
**a result reported after choosing among defensible constructions has already spent degrees of
freedom that no `t`-statistic in the paper accounts for.**

**Dispersion is 68–96% of the premium, and the definition of the variable sits OUTSIDE the published
grids** — so published figures are a **lower bound** on what an outside implementer hits.
