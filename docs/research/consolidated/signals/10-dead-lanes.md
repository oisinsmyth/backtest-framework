# Dead-lane register — every signal territory the two campaigns ran, and the number it died on

[← signals index](00-index.md) · prev: [intraday and overnight](09-intraday-and-overnight.md)

> **NOTHING HERE IS CLOSED.** Under [R15](../../../RULES.md#r15) **only the principal closes an
> avenue.** This is a register of *what each lane recommended and on which number* — so the next
> campaign does not re-commission a lane that already died, and so a revived lane starts from the
> number that killed it.

**The design that produced this table:** every lane is commissioned with a **premise number** — a
count, a rate, an `n_eff` — that must be obtainable **before any return is measured.** **Signal
territories die on that number.** Method and data lanes have no premise number to die on, which is
why they outproduced the signal lanes in every round.

## The pattern, stated once, because it is the most reusable thing here

**Six rounds, thirty-seven briefs, zero strategies — and twenty-one things the programme was wrong
about.** The slate moved `4+2` → `3+3` → `2+4` on signal-versus-method **each time on yield.**

> **That is not a disappointing result reported apologetically. It is the actual yield, consistent
> across six independent rounds — so it is the expected shape, not an anomaly.**
