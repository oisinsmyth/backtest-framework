# Construction dispersion — the detail nobody's name carries decides the answer

[← signals index](00-index.md) · prev: [long leg vs short leg](02-long-leg-versus-short-leg.md) · next: [decay and era](04-decay-and-era.md)

**The finding.** There is a named literature — **non-standard errors** — that measures how much a
result moves across *defensible* construction choices. **The dispersion runs 68–96% of the premium
itself.**

**And this campaign then demonstrated it on itself.** [`F1`](02-long-leg-versus-short-leg.md) is two
of its own lanes, same question, same era, both fully controlled, opposite verdicts — differing on
definition, dataset and cut. *"`C2`'s central finding happening to this campaign in real time rather
than being read about."*

---

## The size of it `[EXT]` `C2`

- **Construction dispersion is 68–96% of the premium.**
- **The definition of the variable sits OUTSIDE the published grids** — so published non-standard
  errors are a **LOWER BOUND** on what this programme hits.
- There is a **published taxonomy** of node types, and round 2 found **one of each** without knowing
  the taxonomy existed.

**The commissioner's overlap audit had returned zero hits for `non-standard error` and `Menkveld`, and
he recorded that as clearance.** It was not: *"a zero hit count means the lane does not overlap. It
does not mean the question is new."* **Already carried in memory as a standing rule.**

## What this implies for anything built here

1. **A result is not a number, it is a number plus a construction.** `C2` §6 sets out the honest
   inversion and the arguments against each remedy — **read it before proposing a fix.**
2. **`C2` §7 joins construction dispersion to multiple testing** — the two are the same problem
   counted differently. → [replication and multiple testing](../method/03-replication-and-multiple-testing.md)
3. **`C2`'s *"what I did not open"* list was used as a work queue by `D1` in the next round**, which
   opened two items and **upgraded one of `C2`'s weakest citations from summariser-sourced to
   read-in-full.** *The cheapest requirement in the depth mandate, and it paid in a second round.*

## And the correction the commissioner made to his own summary

`C2`'s *"the deflator ranked FIRST at \|Δt\| 3.91"* was **a corporate-finance leverage regression's
denominator, not the profitability deflator.** `D3` found the right rows in Table 7: assets → book
equity **12.31**, current → lagged **6.73**. *"`C2`'s heading was accurate; its mapping over-claimed;
my summary repeated it."*
