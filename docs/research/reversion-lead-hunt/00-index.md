# `docs/research/reversion-lead-hunt/` — the reversion lead hunt of 2026-10-02

**What this folder is.** The verbatim records of two debating agent pairs (Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5; one conservative
and one creative agent each), asked by the principal for five solid leads toward a NEW type of effect that predicts
reversion at micro size. **The record of the hunt, the principal's rulings and the main session's checks is
[D772](../../decisions/D772-LEADS-new-reversion-predictors-from-two-agent-pairs.md).** Read it first.

**What it is not.** Nothing here is a result, a decision or a book entry. It closes nothing, admits nothing and
pre-registers nothing. Every lead needs its own pre-registration before its runner exists (R8), and only the
principal opens or closes an avenue (R15). The numbers are the agents' quick in-sample premise tests (2016–2023).
