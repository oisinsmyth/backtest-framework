# What 355 decisions found: D190 – D544

**Written 2026-09-18 ([D545](../decisions/D545-the-narrative-layer-over-D190-D544.md)). Hand-written, unlike every other `.md` in this directory, and dated for that reason.** Every number below is
pinned to a committed artifact by `tests/unit/test_narrative_D190_D544.py`; where a number has no
artifact, that is said out loud.

**Nothing here is at capital.** [`docs/BOOK.md`](../BOOK.md) holds two personal arms not at size and
[`docs/BOOK_PROP.md`](../BOOK_PROP.md) holds one. That is the honest headline, and it is at the top
rather than the bottom because a reader who learns it last has been managed.

What follows is worth a quant's time anyway, because the reason nothing booked is specific and
measurable each time — a cohort exposure, a fill convention, a cost line — and because three of the
six findings are about whether to believe the other three.

## Three findings about trading

### The cohort was the strategy, and the margin was one trade

A long book on "winners that dipped" earned **+160.55 bp per trade** over 3,932 trades on 796 names
at 99.97% exposure. Against a shuffled-path null it looked decisive.

Against **B_c** — the same day, the same top-momentum-decile cohort, a different name — the null
centres at **+126.54** and its p95 is **+155.60**. The book clears by **+4.95**, about 4.6 SE. Then:

- One position — **GME, entered 2021-01-04 at $17.25, held 40 bars, +40,029 bp** — is **6.34% of the
  entire ledger's P&L** and worth **+10.2 bp** of the observed mean, against a margin of +4.95.
  Drop it and the book scores **+150.41, below B_c's p95**. Drop all ten GME trades: **+151.72**,
  still below.
- Two books sharing **nothing but cohort membership** correlate at **ρ = +0.923** (D376).
- Subtract the cohort's own equal-weight return and the gross mean collapses **+160.55 → +34.08**,
  the median to **−30.74** (D377).

**What it says:** a null that lets the arm keep being paid for membership of the decile is not
testing the selector. The avenue was retired.
[D373](../decisions/D373-RESULT-the-winners-dip-is-the-retired-book-and-one-GME-trade.md).

### The fill convention was worth more than the spread

Forty studies computed a signal at the close of bar *t−1* and then earned close[t−1] → close[t] — a
fill at the signal's own close, which no book can do.

Priced properly, the best cell's net went **+18.93 → +5.14 bp/bar, a 73% cut**, and its per-trade
figure went **+10.02 → −18.27**. The overnight gap it had been crediting to every entry was
**+44.8 bp on the long leg and +27.2 on the short** — both **larger than the published half-spread on
either leg**. Breakeven half-spread fell from **80.3 bp (2.48×) to 44.8 (1.42×)**.

**What it says:** a simulator's timing convention is part of the cost model and is declared like
one. [D340](../decisions/D340-RESULT-the-same-close-fill-was-three-quarters-of-the-best-book.md).

### The fee and the barrier are one constraint

A funded futures account has a trailing drawdown of a fixed **$2,000–$4,500**. A full NQ contract's
daily σ is **$705–$1,085** — so **the barrier is three to six daily σ from the running high**. The
micro sits about forty σ away, lives for years, and loses on the fee.

Across **448 lifecycle cells** — 14 plans × two roots × two sizes × four contract counts × two
providers, 8,000 paths each — **none clears**. Going up in size fixes 86% of the micro's cost
problem (NQ last-30: **−0.13 → +0.48** net Sharpe) and then dies at the barrier: **0% alive at 600
days on all 14 plans**.

**What it says:** size is not a lever you can pull twice. The requirement is a daily Sharpe near 0.1
*at whatever size makes the fee small*.
[D493](../decisions/D493-RESULT-the-account-size-lever-fixes-the-fee-and-runs-into-the.md).

## Three findings about whether to believe them

### 93% of an apparent edge was a one-bar bug in a filter

Every look-ahead guard in this framework points at the function that builds the book, and that
function was correct. The defect sat one layer above it, where an **already-lagged** book is
*filtered* by an **unlagged** score.

The score is **98.05% the same number one bar earlier**; the entire result lived in the other 1.95%.
Its correlation with the same bar's return is **+0.0737**; lagged, **−0.0103**.

| | published | corrected |
|---|---:|---:|
| `S1_short|top25` excess Sharpe | **+2.2498** | **−0.6376** |
| CAGR | +1.43% | −0.32% |
| surviving cells | 2 | **0** |

Against a turnover-matched control the ranking is worth **+0.203** Sharpe; the contaminated version
claimed **+3.083**. **Both artifacts are committed side by side** —
`data/d279_concentrated_summary.WITHDRAWN_lookahead.json` and its replacement — because a withdrawn
number that leaves no trace is indistinguishable from one that was never wrong.
[D279](../decisions/D279-the-concentrated-short-on-dead-inclusive-names.md).

### A sampled p95 is biased toward the centre

A null threshold estimated from a finite number of draws is **systematically easier to beat than the
true one**, because an extreme sample quantile sits inside the extreme it estimates.

Where the null's group is finite and small — a time rotation of one market-level series is about
4,000 offsets — it can be **enumerated instead of sampled**, and the sampling error becomes exactly
zero. Done for all four cells of a published study:

| | published p95, 200 draws | exact p95 | margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| G1:T1 | +56.54 | +56.82 | −61.29 |
| G1:T2 | +35.04 | +36.08 | −20.51 |
| G2:T1 | +61.60 | +68.51 | −64.33 |
| **G2:T2** | +35.95 | **+41.81** | **+0.52** |

**All four exact values are higher.** The mean shift on the p50 is −0.31 and mixed in sign; on the
p95 it is **+3.52 and positive in four of four**. No verdict changed — but G2:T2's published margin
of **+6.4 collapsed to +0.52**, and it was the only one thin enough to be at risk.

**The cure is one corner.** Per-name rotations, block bootstraps and the rest are not enumerable;
there the bias stands and only more draws touch it. Recorded in `data/c2b_rotation_audit.json` with
`se = 0.0` by construction.

*(Distinct from [D373](../decisions/D373-the-winners-dip-long-and-the-median-criterion.md)'s rule,
which is that every reported p95 carries its bootstrap SE and any margin within **2 SE** of its bar
is recorded UNRESOLVED rather than passed. One is a theorem about sampling; the other is a
reporting standard. They are not the same claim.)*

### Precision cannot detect bias

A test of "does the book survive losing its ten best names" ran at 10,000 draws and failed the book
at **−15.8 SE** — a confident, tightly-bounded, wrong answer. The null removed the *same* ten names
from every draw, so only the observed book paid the penalty.

Make the null symmetric — each draw loses **its own** ten — and the verdict reverses:

| | draws | p50 | p95 | observed | margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| asymmetric | 10,000 | +1.562 | +4.230 | +3.919 | **−0.311** |
| **symmetric** | 2,000 | +0.679 | +3.617 | **+4.219** | **+0.602** |

**−15.8 SE to +10.3 SE**, on one fifth of the draws. A draw's own ten overlap the observed book's on
a median of 5 of 10, so the old test was unfair rather than absurd — and no number of draws would
have revealed it.

**What it says:** more draws make a biased estimator more *precisely* biased. Draw count is a
precision decision; null symmetry is a correctness one.
[D370](../decisions/D370-RESULT-the-symmetric-top-ten-removal.md).

## What the programme has not got

**No component clears the prop standard.** Six constructions scored at the instrument's minimum
tradable size, under the $3 round trip that size actually pays: best is **+0.369 net Sharpe
(SE 0.322)**, second is **−0.011 (SE 0.397)**, and the assembled book is **+0.031**. The ledger's
`entered` list is empty.

**The numbers that opened that ledger were wrong, and they have no artifact.** Figures of **0.62 and
0.42** at correlation 0.09, and a **0.91** book, were computed outside the runner in basis points at
full-size cost. At the standard's own size and cost line they are **+0.37 and −0.01**, and there is
no book. Only the corrected pair exists in `data/d466_components.json`; the originals survive solely
as a confession inside
[D466](../decisions/D466-RESULT-no-construction-clears-the-component-standard-at.md).

**That asymmetry is the finding.** A number that never passed through a runner cannot be sourced,
cannot be gated, and gets believed anyway — it reached `CLAUDE.md` and the working notes before
anyone re-ran it. Every figure above this paragraph has a key in `data/`. Those three do not, and
they are the three that were wrong.
