A correction, and it changed a number by more than half

The first version of this page admitted a volume entry on every qualifying bar. Consecutive top-quintile bars share seven of their eight bars, so it reported 43,204 “trades” from four names over 8.25 years — 1,309 per symbol per year against 252 sessions. A book cannot open 1,300 positions in 252 sessions; those were overlapping observations wearing a trade’s clothes.

Held non-overlapping, as a book would: 13,752 trades, mean +1.57 bp, and the t-statistic falls from +5.30 to +2.31 — to roughly 1.6 once the four names’ cross-correlation is accounted for, since they carry only 1.87 effective instruments.

And the symmetry is the tell. The percentile ratios sit at 1.04, 1.08 and 1.07 across p25/p75, p5/p95 and p1/p99 — a constant tilt at every point. That is a location shift, not a change of shape; a real edge fattens the winning tail relative to the losing one and the ratio grows outward. Against the unconditional distribution the conditioning moves the mean +2.15 bp and widens the spread from 58.8 to 77.2 bp. High relative volume is mostly selecting volatility; direction is a side effect riding on it. The cost bar is 4.00 bp.

What the shape means

Kurtosis between 10.7 and 24.5. A normal distribution scores 3. These are violently fat-tailed, which is why a mean computed over them is fragile.

Two of the three are positively skewed — +0.19 and +1.14. In a short book that is the wrong direction: it means the profit arrives as rare large gains rather than as a steady harvest, and rare large gains are exactly what a backtest cannot promise will recur.

Median P&L is zero or negative in all three — the typical trade makes nothing. Hit rates cluster at 47.7–50.1% with payoff ratios of 1.08–1.19, so on the body of the distribution these are coin flips with almost no asymmetry.

The by-year bars show no era carrying it either. S2's mean per trade falls from +17.2 bp in 2018 to −6.1 in 2025; S1's is lumpy rather than trending. Neither looks like a stable process observed over eight years.
